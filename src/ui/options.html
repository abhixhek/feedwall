Feedwall

Your feed, your rules. Say what you want more of and what you want gone. Everything stays one click away.

Your TypeSafe API key

Feedwall has no server. Post text and your topics go from your browser straight to TypeSafe under your own key, and nowhere else. The key is stored only in this browser.

About you (optional)

One or two lines of background. It is sent once with each post, so every topic can take it into account.

Your topics

Each topic is a question asked of every post. Choose what happens when a post fits. Keep always wins, then Hide, then Dim, then Highlight.

New topic

Sets

A set is your "about you" line plus your topics. Start from one of these, or share yours as a code anyone can paste.

How it behaves

Strictness (for topics without their own setting)

It can only filter what the site already loaded, so a focused feed is shorter and you will scroll more.

Daily request limit (filtering pauses when reached)

How is it doing?

Built from your "Right", "Wrong" and "This post fits…" marks. A topic needs about 8 marks before Feedwall suggests a change.

Recently hidden

Mark a few now and then. It is how Feedwall learns where your line is.

Your data

Feedwall keeps the last 200 posts you scrolled past (text only, in this browser) so "Test it" has something real to run on. Exports are in jevcal's format, for computing exact thresholds.
